## Summary of Work

`scripts/smolvla_simulation_benchmark.py` loads the pinned policy and the LIBERO environment, runs the full benchmark, and writes three reproducible artifacts: `artifacts/smolvla_simulation_benchmark.json` (full config snapshot, per-episode rows, per-condition summaries with Wilson intervals, offline scores, correlation with disagreements, acceptance checks, and a per-condition failure analysis), a standalone `artifacts/smolvla_simulation_benchmark_config.json` for rerunning the identical experiment, and `artifacts/smolvla_simulation_benchmark.png` (success rate with Wilson CI, action deviation, and query latency per condition). Videos are intentionally omitted: LIBERO episode videos are large, and the quantitative per-episode failure analysis covers behavior; the decision is recorded in the artifact claim scope and `docs/LEROBOT_INTEGRATION.md`.
